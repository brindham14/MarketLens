Frequently asked questions

The basics, in short.

What is relative strength (RS) in stocks?

Relative strength compares a stock's price performance with every other stock over the same period. Here each stock is ranked from 1 to 99 (99 is strongest) using a weighted mix of 3, 6, 9 and 12-month returns. Stocks with a high RS rating have been leading the market.

Is Market Strength Scanner free?

This prototype is free to open. Live data and any provider access may require separate configuration or subscription.

Which markets does it cover?

Indian stocks, designed around a broad Nifty 500-style universe. Current bundled prices and rankings are demo data.

How often is the data updated?

The interface can be connected to update after each trading day's close. The quote sheet can provide daily price changes, but technical rankings and charts require a verified historical OHLCV data feed.

Is this investment advice?

No. It is an educational and research tool. Rankings, screens and backtests are mechanical outputs and are not recommendations to buy, sell or hold securities.
